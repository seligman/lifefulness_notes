# Life Full On

My notes on what happened to Sanderson Jones' podcast *Lifefulness: Live Life Fully*.  It went quiet in October 2021, then in 2026 someone pointed its Apple and Spotify listings at a copy and started filling it with reworded versions of someone else's podcast and some old sermons, under the name "Life Full On".

- [podcast_source.md](podcast_source.md) - Two of the new episodes are not entirely original, they're older sermons by Myles Munroe and Joshua Selman, cut way down and reworded, I'd guess by a robot.  The Selman one credits him right there in its own description, which made that one easy.
- [looking_for_other_marks.md](looking_for_other_marks.md) - Went through about 352,000 podcast feeds looking for other shows this happened to.  Didn't find any.  Plenty of people copy podcasts, they just don't take over the original show's listing.
- [hacking_the_hackers.md](hacking_the_hackers.md) - Finding quiet podcasts takes one query against a free download, or a list starting at a dollar if you'd rather buy one.  I doubt that's how they found Sanderson's though, my guess is they got into his Acast account first and the show just happened to be sitting there.

As of October 14, Apple and Spotify are back on Sanderson's original show.
